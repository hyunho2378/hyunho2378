## Projects

### N9 Hair Salon | 2026.04 ~ 2026.06
> Booking and an AI hair diagnosis web app for a one-person hair salon in Chuncheon

- **Context**: A salon with 30 years of experience that only took reservations by phone.
- **What I built**: Naver Booking and an automatic reply on Naver TalkTalk, plus a web app that recommends one of 50 treatment cards from 8 questions.
- **Results**: Naver Place visits went from 75 in March to 511 in May. 15 Naver bookings by September 29, 118 AI diagnosis uses, and 17 new male customers. A six-month maintenance contract followed.
- **Links**: [Repository](https://github.com/hyunho2378/n9_service_design_project) / [Live](https://n9-service-design-project.vercel.app)

---

### Gangneung Pay UX Improvement | 2026.03 ~ 2026.06
> Redesign of a local payment app in a team of six

- **Role**: UX research, UI design and front-end development. My share of the work was 40% of research, 80% of UI and 100% of front-end.
- **Research**: Survey of 70 people, 6 interviews, 4 observations, and the real data of 13,021 member stores.
- **Result**: Every participant in the usability test completed the tasks.
- **Links**: [Repository](https://github.com/hyunho2378/Gangneung-Pay) / [Live](https://gangneung-pay.vercel.app)

### Donghae Sai | 2026.08 ~
> A stay-and-explore guide service that turns day visitors into overnight guests

- **Context**: Hallym G-Lab@Donghae Adventure, Team 2, together with the research team. A first proposal went to the city and follow-up talks are going on.
- **Role**: Branding and service planning, the guide AI, and the web. Designed the logo and the character Muko.
- **Research**: Survey of 77 people and 3 expert interviews.
- **Design**: A local AI that reads only the city's own materials and runs on the city's own server, so data stays inside.
- **Links**: [Repository](https://github.com/hyunho2378/Donghae-sai) / [Live](https://donghae-sai.vercel.app)
