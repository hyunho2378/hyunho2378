# Hyun Ho Ju

Service designer and front-end developer in Korea.
I connect what a region already has to the people who need it, through AI and design.

## What I work on

Most regions already hold the stories, places and records that people look for. What is broken is the path between those resources and the person who needs them. I work on that path, from field research to the screens people actually use.

Right now that work is in three places. In Donghae, a guide AI that reads only the city's own materials. In Jeongseon, scattered official information organized around the lyrics of Jeongseon Arirang. In Chuncheon, a character brand set around the Chuncheon Mime Festival.
